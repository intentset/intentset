# Visitors learn Intentset the way they learn best

## Rationale

Some people learn by reading the documentation through, others by asking questions, and a reader in the middle of a specification often has a question the page in front of them does not answer. intentset.org has only offered reading. A chat that answers from the published documentation, and links each answer to the page it came from, serves the second kind of reader without asking the first to change.

Intentset is suited to this: publication already decides, failing closed, which knowledge an audience may see, and keeps the sources of every published document. The chat answers from that and nothing else.
